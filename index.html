Ratios use equal temperament, so most of these scales never realign exactly — they drift in and out of phase, occasionally landing close ("near-alignment"). That drift is itself the honest picture: real musical intervals like a major third or a fifth are only close to simple whole-number ratios on a piano, not exact.
